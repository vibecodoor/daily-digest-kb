## Sell the workflow, not the wrapper

**Insight:** buyers pay for a complete workflow that removes their friction, not
for a thin UI over a model they could call themselves.

**Evidence:** illustrative — observed pattern across solo-builder products where
the durable sellers package an end-to-end loop, not raw model access. (Flag:
anecdotal; revisit with real data.)

**How to apply:**

- Package the full loop (input → result the buyer would pay for).
- Price on the outcome, not on tokens.
